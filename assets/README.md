# Asset notes

## Illustration

`cozy-desk.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then converted to WebP for efficient static delivery. The original generated file is preserved outside the repository.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a standalone illustration for the hero of a cozy personal coding portfolio, no interface or text. Primary request: a quiet, inviting wooden desk beside a large window looking onto leafy trees at golden hour. An open laptop with a simple dark green screen, a terracotta ceramic mug with a little steam, a small potted plant, two books, and a warm desk lamp. No people. Style: sophisticated hand-painted gouache and colored-pencil editorial illustration, slightly imperfect ink outlines, flat shapes, tactile subtle paper grain, charming restrained details, not photorealistic, not 3D. Composition: landscape 4:3, full scene filling the frame with the desk across the lower half, the window in the upper center, balanced and calm, suitable for a rounded rectangular crop. Palette: forest green and olive foliage, honey ochre light, warm walnut wood, cream paper, restrained terracotta. Mood: warm, peaceful, lived-in, like a little place to make things. Constraints: no legible text, no logos, no watermark, no UI chrome, no border or surrounding page.

## Fonts

DM Sans and Lora are self-hosted variable fonts from the Google Fonts repository, converted to WOFF2. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. The complete license texts are in `fonts/DM-Sans-OFL.txt` and `fonts/Lora-OFL.txt`.

- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Lora: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lora

`favicon.svg` is a simple initial mark. `grain.svg` provides a subtle paper texture. The small interface icons are inline SVG in `index.html`.
